Anyone can deploy a token called NVDA. A person, or a bot, who copies a wallet sees a ticker and a log. copy-guard reads the evidence first, sizes a practice trade by how strong that evidence is, and writes down every reason in plain sentences. Nothing on this page asks you to trust me. The last section shows how to recheck it.

Ledger health

Decision cards

Each card starts with a score, how many of the 18 checks passed, and a mark for each group of checks. A red flag is a check that did not pass. Each card is built from fixed sentences filled with the values the rules read. No language model writes or decides anything, so the same inputs always give the same card. The card is part of the record whose hash is onchain.

The practice ledger

Two books walk through the same moves in the order they happened. copy-guard opens what its rules, its mandate and the user's limits allow. Blind copy opens the full size on everything that looks like a Stock Token arriving. Both enter at the Chainlink feed's value at the block of the move and leave by the same exit rules.

Who set the limits, and who the agent acts for

The user's risk limits (limits.json)

The mandate (mandate/)

One sentence, two values. A person who says “put at most $50 on one stock and stop the loss at 15%” changes two lines of limits.json: "full_usd": "50.00" and "stop_loss_pct": "15". The file is edited by hand, there is no parser for the sentence. Every later decision names the new file by hash, and the mandate has to be signed again, because new limits need the principal's consent.

These three headings are the words the Blockchain Legal Institute uses for its planned AI Agent Registration Tool.

Chainlink CRE: a compliance check before the agent acts

Three CRE workflows sit inside the decision. They are run with cre workflow simulate. Nothing is deployed to a DON, and a simulated write goes through CRE's simulation forwarder, which accepts reports from anyone. The events fix a hash and a time. They are not a DON attestation, which is why the recheck exists.

The questions regulators are asking, and what this keeps

I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice. The table does not say what any rule requires or what a court would accept. It lists words that rule makers and regulators have used, next to the material this build leaves behind.

SourceTheir wordsWhat this build keeps
U.S. Federal Rule of Evidence 902(14)data copied from an electronic file, “authenticated by a process of digital identification”Every record and every decision is named by a keccak256 hash that anyone can recompute. This is the kind of check the rule has in mind. The rule also asks for a qualified person's certification, which a script does not replace.
Rule 902(13)a record “generated by an electronic process or system that produces an accurate result”The rules are fixed and use no language model. Anyone can rerun them on the saved inputs and compare.

FINRA's concern is agents that act without predefined rules. This agent is the opposite case: its rules are predefined.

  2. Open the ledger's root on Robinhood Testnet: . One LedgerAnchored event carries the Merkle root of every decision on this page.
  3. Open the payment on Robinhood Chain: 0x65c314bd…87af61. 0.01 USDG from the agent's wallet to vet402's wallet, 2026-10-08. I paid it myself as a test. Both wallets are mine.
  4. Open the anchor on Robinhood Chain: 0x15f9ed8a…585c7f. One Anchored log with the hash of the demo wallet's record.

What this does not claim

  • Practice (paper) trades only. No real trade was placed, and nothing here says that copying any wallet makes money.
  • The ledger compares two policies on past public moves. It is not a forecast and not advice.
  • The walk is a replay. Each wallet's record was read once, after the moves, so the rules judge a wallet with what is known now.
  • Feed values arrive a few times a day. An entry price can be hours older than the move, within the age limit the user set.
  • No customer yet. The one payment so far is my own test.
  • vet402 reports what public chain data shows. It gives no legal or investment advice and is not affiliated with Robinhood, Chainlink or any token issuer.
